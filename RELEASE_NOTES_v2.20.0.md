# Portable Notes 2.28.0: Risk Radar

This cumulative release adds deterministic task-risk scoring on top of due dates, priorities, and dependency metadata.

## New
- `/api/task-risk` with optional project filtering
- Risk scoring for overdue, high-priority, blocked, and unresolved-dependency work
- Critical, High, Medium, and Low classifications
- Human-readable risk reasons
- New Risk Radar command

## Retained
All v2.19 and earlier notes, search, knowledge tools, task/project/milestone workflows, dependencies, critical path, history, attachments, export, templates, daily notes, custom vault and privacy functionality remains.
